What the model wrote against what the catalog says

70 test specimen sheets. Each model saw only the image and the frozen v2 prompt. Each AI value is compared with the curated GBIF record and scored with the pre-registered rules. A disagreement is not always an AI error: the record can carry information that is not on the label.

Match covers exact and normalized. Unsupported means the AI filled a field the record leaves empty: an upper bound on hallucination until the image audit. Omitted means the AI returned null where the record has a value. Images load from the SERNEC portal (CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-NC 4.0; the licence is shown on each card) and may take a moment.
